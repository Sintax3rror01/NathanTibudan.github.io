Signature Mains

Tonkotsu Ramen

Ingredients

  • 2 bundles Fresh Ramen noodles
  • 600ml Creamy Pork Bone Broth
  • 4 slices Chashu (Braised Pork Belly)
  • 1 Marinated Soft Egg (Ajitsuke Tamago)
  • 10g Nori, 20g Scallions, 30g Menma

Process

  1. Heat your broth in a saucepan until it reaches a low boil. Maintain heat.
  2. Boil 1 liter of water. Cook fresh noodles for 90-120 seconds. Drain thoroughly.
  3. Pour 1 tbsp of soy-based tare (seasoning) into a bowl, followed by the hot broth.
  4. Place noodles in the center. Top with sliced pork, half-cut egg, and nori. Serve hot.

Chicken Katsu

Ingredients

  • 2 Chicken breasts (pounded to 1cm)
  • 100g Flour, 1 Large Egg, 150g Panko
  • 5g Salt, 2g Black Pepper
  • 300ml Neutral frying oil
  • Katsu Sauce and shredded cabbage

Process

  1. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Set up 3 bowls: flour, beaten egg, and panko.
  2. Dredge chicken in flour, dip in egg, and press firmly into panko for full coverage.
  3. Heat oil to 175°C. Fry chicken for 4 mins per side until deep golden brown.
  4. Rest on a wire rack for 3 minutes. Slice into strips and serve with cabbage.

Beef Gyudon

Ingredients

  • 300g Paper-thin Beef (Ribeye or Chuck)
  • 1 medium White Onion (thinly sliced)
  • 120ml Dashi, 30ml Soy Sauce, 15ml Mirin
  • 10g Sugar, 2 bowls Steamed Rice

Process

  1. Combine dashi, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar in a pan. Bring to a boil.
  2. Add sliced onions and simmer over medium heat for 5-7 minutes until soft.
  3. Add beef slices. Cook for only 2 minutes—do not overcook to keep it tender.
  4. Ladle beef and a generous amount of juice over rice. Top with pickled ginger.

Sushi Platter

Ingredients

  • 300g Sushi Rice (Short-grain)
  • 45ml Rice Vinegar, 10g Sugar
  • 200g Sashimi-grade Salmon or Tuna
  • 4 Nori sheets, 1 Cucumber
  • Wasabi and Soy Sauce

Process

  1. Rinse rice 3 times. Cook and mix with vinegar/sugar while warm. Let cool.
  2. Place Nori on bamboo mat. Spread 150g rice, leaving 2cm at the top.
  3. Lay fish and cucumber strips in the center. Roll tightly using the mat.
  4. Using a very sharp, wet knife, slice into 8 pieces. Serve with wasabi.

Appetizers

Pork Gyoza

Ingredients

  • 20 Gyoza wrappers
  • 250g Ground Pork, 100g Minced Cabbage
  • 5g Grated Ginger, 1 clove Garlic
  • 10ml Sesame Oil, 10ml Soy Sauce

Process

  1. Salt cabbage, sit for 10 mins, and squeeze out water. Mix with pork and seasonings.
  2. Place 1 tsp filling in wrapper. Wet edges and pleat one side to seal.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil. Fry gyoza until bottoms are brown.
  4. Add 60ml water, cover immediately. Steam for 3 mins, then cook off remaining water.

Octopus Takoyaki

Ingredients

  • 200g Takoyaki Flour, 600ml Water, 1 Egg
  • 150g Boiled Octopus (diced)
  • Tenkasu (Tempura bits), Green Onion
  • Takoyaki Sauce, Mayo, Bonito Flakes

Process

  1. Whisk flour, water, and egg into a very thin batter. Preheat takoyaki pan.
  2. Pour batter to the top of holes. Drop octopus and bits into each.
  3. As the bottom sets, use a pick to flip 90 degrees. Add more batter to fill gaps.
  4. Continue rotating until they are round and brown. Serve with sauce and flakes.

Salted Edamame

Ingredients

  • 400g Fresh Edamame pods
  • 20g Sea salt (coarse)
  • 2L Water

Process

  1. Boil 2L of water with 10g of salt.
  2. Add edamame pods and boil for 4-5 minutes until bright green and tender.
  3. Drain well. Do not rinse with cold water—the steam helps salt stick.
  4. Toss with remaining 10g of salt and serve immediately.

Crispy Spring Rolls

Ingredients

  • 10 Spring Roll wrappers
  • 150g Ground Pork, 100g Shredded Carrots
  • 50g Vermicelli (soaked), 1 tbsp Oyster Sauce
  • 1 Egg (for sealing), Frying Oil

Process

  1. Sauté pork and veggies with oyster sauce. Cool the filling completely.
  2. Place 2 tbsp filling on wrapper corner. Roll tightly, folding sides in.
  3. Brush the edge with egg wash to seal.
  4. Deep fry at 180°C for 3-5 minutes until golden and blistered.

Desserts

Red Bean Mochi

Ingredients

  • 100g Glutinous Rice Flour (Mochiko)
  • 180ml Water, 50g Sugar
  • 200g Sweet Red Bean Paste (Anko)
  • Cornstarch for dusting

Process

  1. Whisk flour, sugar, and water. Cover and microwave for 2 mins.
  2. Stir sticky dough, microwave for 1 more min until translucent.
  3. Place on cornstarch-dusted board. Cut into 8 circles.
  4. Place a ball of bean paste in center and pinch dough over to seal.

Honey Dorayaki

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs, 50g Sugar, 15ml Honey
  • 100g Flour, 2g Baking Soda
  • 30ml Milk, Red Bean Paste

Process

  1. Whisk eggs, sugar, and honey until pale. Fold in flour and baking soda.
  2. Add milk, whisk, and rest batter for 15 minutes.
  3. Cook small pancakes on low heat. Flip when bubbles appear.
  4. Sandwich two pancakes with bean paste.

Custard Taiyaki

Ingredients

  • 150g Cake Flour, 1 Egg, 180ml Milk
  • 5g Baking Powder, 20g Sugar
  • Custard Cream filling

Process

  1. Whisk egg and sugar, add milk and flour. Rest for 30 mins.
  2. Pour batter into preheated fish-mold. Add 1 tbsp custard.
  3. Cover with more batter. Close mold and cook 2 mins per side.

Matcha Pudding

Ingredients

  • 10g Matcha Powder, 400ml Milk
  • 50g Sugar, 10g Gelatin Powder
  • 30ml Warm Water (for gelatin)

Process

  1. Dissolve gelatin in warm water. Heat milk and sugar until simmering.
  2. Whisk matcha with 2 tbsp milk to make a paste, then add to milk.
  3. Stir in gelatin. Pour into cups and chill for 4-6 hours.
