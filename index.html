Signature Mains

Tonkotsu Ramen

Ingredients

  • 2 bundles Fresh Ramen noodles
  • 600ml Creamy Pork Bone Broth
  • 4 slices Chashu (Braised Pork Belly)
  • 1 Marinated Soft Egg (Ajitsuke Tamago)
  • 10g Nori, 20g Scallions, 30g Menma

Process

  1. Heat your broth in a saucepan until it reaches a low boil. Maintain heat.
  2. Boil 1 liter of water. Cook fresh noodles for 90-120 seconds. Drain thoroughly.
  3. Pour 1 tbsp of soy-based tare into a bowl, followed by the hot broth.
  4. Place noodles in the center. Top with sliced pork, half-cut egg, and nori.

Chicken Katsu

Ingredients

  • 2 Chicken breasts (pounded to 1cm)
  • 100g Flour, 1 Large Egg, 150g Panko
  • 5g Salt, 2g Black Pepper
  • 300ml Neutral frying oil

Process

  1. Season chicken. Set up 3 bowls: flour, beaten egg, and panko.
  2. Dredge chicken in flour, dip in egg, and press firmly into panko.
  3. Heat oil to 175°C. Fry chicken for 4 mins per side until golden brown.
  4. Rest on a wire rack for 3 minutes. Slice into strips.

Beef Gyudon

Ingredients

  • 300g Paper-thin Beef (Ribeye or Chuck)
  • 1 medium White Onion (thinly sliced)
  • 120ml Dashi, 30ml Soy Sauce, 15ml Mirin
  • 10g Sugar, 2 bowls Steamed Rice

Process

  1. Combine dashi, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar in a pan. Bring to a boil.
  2. Add sliced onions and simmer over medium heat for 5-7 minutes.
  3. Add beef slices. Cook for only 2 minutes—do not overcook.
  4. Ladle beef and juice over rice. Top with pickled ginger.

Sushi Platter

Ingredients

  • 300g Sushi Rice (Short-grain)
  • 45ml Rice Vinegar, 10g Sugar
  • 200g Sashimi-grade Salmon or Tuna
  • 4 Nori sheets, 1 Cucumber

Process

  1. Rinse rice 3 times. Cook and mix with vinegar/sugar while warm.
  2. Place Nori on bamboo mat. Spread 150g rice, leaving 2cm at the top.
  3. Lay fish and cucumber strips in the center. Roll tightly.
  4. Using a wet knife, slice into 8 pieces. Serve with wasabi.

Appetizers

Pork Gyoza

Ingredients

  • 20 Gyoza wrappers
  • 250g Ground Pork
  • 5g Ginger, 1 clove Garlic
  • Sesame Oil, Soy Sauce

Process

  1. Mix pork and seasonings. Place 1 tsp filling in wrapper.
  2. Heat oil. Fry until bottoms are brown.
  3. Add 60ml water, cover and steam for 3 mins.

Octopus Takoyaki

Ingredients

  • 200g Takoyaki Flour, 1 Egg
  • 150g Octopus (diced)
  • Tenkasu, Green Onion

Process

  1. Whisk batter. Pour into preheated takoyaki pan.
  2. Add octopus. Use a pick to flip 90 degrees as it sets.
  3. Rotate until round and brown. Serve with sauce.

Salted Edamame

Ingredients

  • 400g Fresh Edamame pods
  • 20g Sea salt
  • 2L Water

Process

  1. Boil water with 10g salt.
  2. Boil pods for 4-5 mins. Drain well.
  3. Toss with remaining salt and serve.

Crispy Spring Rolls

Ingredients

  • 10 Spring Roll wrappers
  • 150g Ground Pork, Carrots
  • Vermicelli, Oyster Sauce

Process

  1. Sauté filling. Roll tightly in wrappers.
  2. Brush edge with egg to seal.
  3. Deep fry at 180°C until golden.

Desserts

Red Bean Mochi

Ingredients

  • 100g Glutinous Rice Flour
  • 180ml Water, 50g Sugar
  • 200g Red Bean Paste

Process

  1. Microwave flour/water/sugar mixture for 3 mins total.
  2. Place on cornstarch. Cut into circles.
  3. Wrap dough around bean paste balls.

Honey Dorayaki

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs, 50g Sugar, 15ml Honey
  • 100g Flour, 2g Baking Soda
  • Red Bean Paste

Process

  1. Whisk eggs/sugar/honey. Fold in dry ingredients.
  2. Cook small pancakes on low heat.
  3. Sandwich two pancakes with paste.

Custard Taiyaki

Ingredients

  • 150g Cake Flour, 1 Egg
  • Milk, Baking Powder
  • Custard Cream filling

Process

  1. Whisk batter and rest for 30 mins.
  2. Pour into fish-mold. Add custard.
  3. Close mold and cook 2 mins per side.

Matcha Pudding

Ingredients

  • 10g Matcha, 400ml Milk
  • 50g Sugar, 10g Gelatin

Process

  1. Dissolve gelatin. Heat milk and sugar.
  2. Whisk matcha paste into milk.
  3. Add gelatin, pour into cups and chill.

Refreshing Drinks

Matcha Latte

Ingredients

  • 5g Matcha, 50ml Hot water
  • 200ml Milk
  • Honey to taste

Process

  1. Whisk matcha with water until frothy.
  2. Heat and froth milk.
  3. Pour milk over matcha and sweeten.

Classic Bubble Tea

Ingredients

  • 2 Tea bags, 50g Pearls
  • Brown sugar syrup, Milk

Process

  1. Cook pearls and soak in syrup.
  2. Brew tea and let cool.
  3. Combine pearls, ice, tea, and milk.

Iced Green Tea

Ingredients

  • Loose leaf Green tea
  • Water, Lemon, Ice

Process

  1. Steep tea in 80°C water for 3 mins.
  2. Strain and let cool.
  3. Pour over ice and garnish with lemon.

Yuzu Ginger Soda

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp Yuzu juice
  • Sparkling water, Ginger
  • Mint leaves

Process

  1. Mix yuzu and ginger in glass.
  2. Add ice and sparkling water.
  3. Garnish with fresh mint.
